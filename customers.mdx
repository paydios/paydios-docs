PkPay+ automatically creates a customer record for every person who completes a payment through your checkout. You can view customer profiles, browse their full payment history, and access contact and billing details directly from the merchant dashboard at [https://merchant.pkpayplus.com/](https://merchant.pkpayplus.com/). No manual setup is required — records are created at the time of the first successful transaction.

## Viewing customers

The **Customers** section of your dashboard lists all customers associated with your merchant account. Each row in the list shows:

- **Name** and **email address**
- **Total spend** across all transactions
- **Last transaction date**
- **Customer status** (active, inactive)

Use the search bar to find a customer by name or email address. You can also filter the list by date range or customer status to narrow down results when managing a large customer base.

## Customer profile

Click any customer in the list to open their full profile. Each profile page includes the following information:

- **Contact details** — full name, email address, and billing address on file
- **Payment history** — a complete log of all transactions, including amounts, currencies, dates, and statuses (`paid`, `pending`, `failed`, `refunded`, `disputed`)
- **Saved payment methods** — card details or wallet tokens stored if the customer opted in during checkout
- **Active subscriptions** — any recurring billing plans the customer is currently enrolled in
- **Invoices** — all invoices issued to that customer, with their current payment status

From the profile page you can click into any individual transaction to view its full detail, or navigate directly to a linked subscription or invoice.

## Customer data and privacy

PkPay+ stores only the payment and contact data necessary to process and record transactions. All stored data is protected under PCI DSS Level 1 compliance, and payment card data is tokenized so raw card numbers are never held in PkPay+ systems.

Under GDPR, customers in applicable regions have the right to request deletion of their personal data. If a customer contacts you with a data deletion request, reach out to PkPay+ support so the request can be handled in compliance with applicable regulations. Deleting a customer record does not alter historical transaction records required for financial compliance.

## Exporting customer data

To export your customer list, navigate to **Customers** in the dashboard, apply any filters you need (such as a date range or status), and click **Export**. PkPay+ generates a CSV file containing the filtered customer records, which you can download for use in CRM tools, marketing platforms, or internal reporting.
