Every time a customer completes a checkout session through your PkPay+ integration, a customer profile is created or updated in your merchant account. These profiles give you a consolidated view of each buyer's contact information, transaction history, and payment activity — all accessible from the dashboard without writing any code.

## What is stored in a customer profile

PkPay+ automatically captures and stores the following information when a customer makes a payment:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contact Information" icon="address-card">
    Name, email address, and billing address as entered during checkout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment History" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A chronological list of every transaction the customer has completed through your account, including amounts, currencies, statuses, and dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transaction Totals" icon="chart-bar">
    Lifetime spend, number of successful payments, and any refunded or disputed amounts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dispute and Refund Activity" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Any chargebacks, refunds, or disputes associated with the customer's payments on your account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Viewing customers in the dashboard

Log in to [merchant.pkpayplus.com](https://merchant.pkpayplus.com) and navigate to **Customers** in the left sidebar. The customer list shows:

- Customer name and email
- Date of first payment
- Total number of transactions
- Total lifetime spend
- Most recent transaction date and status

Click any customer row to open the full customer profile with their complete transaction history.

## Searching and filtering customers

Use the search bar at the top of the customer list to find a specific customer by name or email address. You can also filter the list by:

- **Date range** — show only customers who made their first purchase within a given period
- **Transaction status** — filter to customers with at least one `paid`, `refunded`, or `disputed` transaction
- **Spend range** — narrow the list to customers above or below a lifetime spend threshold

## Customer payment history

The payment history tab inside a customer profile lists every transaction in reverse chronological order. Each row shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Transaction ID** | Unique identifier for the payment |
| **Date** | Date and time the payment was initiated |
| **Amount** | Payment amount and currency |
| **Status** | Current status: `paid`, `pending`, `failed`, `refunded`, or `disputed` |
| **Session ID** | The checkout session that generated the transaction |

Click any transaction row to open the full transaction detail view, where you can issue a refund or view dispute information if applicable.

## Issuing a refund from a customer profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer profile">
    Navigate to **Customers**, find the customer, and click their name to open the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the transaction">
    Under **Payment history**, click the transaction you want to refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate the refund">
    Click **Refund** in the transaction detail panel. Choose a full or partial refund amount and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the refund">
    The transaction status updates to `refunded` (full) or `partial_refund`. The customer receives the funds according to their payment method's standard processing time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Refunds are deducted from your available PkPay+ balance. If your balance is insufficient, the refund amount becomes an outstanding balance owed to PkPay+ and is recovered from future settlements.
</Note>

## Customer data and privacy

PkPay+ stores customer data only as needed to process payments, manage your merchant account, and comply with legal obligations.

<Accordion title="What data is retained and for how long?">
  PkPay+ retains transaction records and associated customer data for the period required to satisfy payment-network chargeback windows, legal obligations, and dispute resolution requirements. This is typically a minimum of five years for transaction records. Contact [help@paydios.io](mailto:help@paydios.io) for specific data-retention questions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Handling customer data deletion requests">
  If a customer submits a data deletion or erasure request under applicable privacy law, contact [legal@paydios.io](mailto:legal@paydios.io). PkPay+ will review the request against its legal retention obligations and respond accordingly. Note that transaction records required for compliance, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention cannot be deleted early.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Exporting customer data">
  To export a customer's complete transaction history, open the customer profile in the dashboard and click **Export** in the top-right corner. The export is delivered as a CSV file to the email address associated with your merchant account.
</Accordion>

## Tips for managing customer data quality

<Tip>
  Encourage customers to use a consistent email address across purchases. PkPay+ uses email address as the primary identifier when linking transactions to a customer profile — different email addresses result in separate profiles even for the same person.
</Tip>
