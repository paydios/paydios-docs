PkPay+ invoicing lets you create and send professional, branded invoices directly from the dashboard without any additional software. Each invoice includes an embedded one-click payment link, so customers can pay in seconds using any supported method. When payment clears, the invoice reconciles automatically — no manual bookkeeping required.

## Creating an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Invoicing in the dashboard">
    Log in to [merchant.pkpayplus.com](https://merchant.pkpayplus.com/) and select **Invoicing** from the sidebar. Click **New Invoice** to open the invoice editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add customer details">
    Enter the customer's name, email address, and billing address. PkPay+ stores customer profiles so you can auto-fill returning customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Click **Add Line Item** for each product or service. For each line, provide:
    - **Description** — what you're billing for
    - **Quantity** — number of units
    - **Unit price** — price per unit
    PkPay+ calculates totals and any applicable tax automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set due date and currency">
    Choose the invoice currency (any of the 135+ supported currencies) and set a payment due date. PkPay+ uses the due date to trigger automated reminders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise branding">
    Upload your logo, choose brand colours, and add a custom message or footer. Your invoice will reflect your business identity, not PkPay+'s.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invoice">
    Click **Send via Email** to deliver the invoice directly to the customer's inbox, or click **Copy Link** to share the invoice URL manually through any channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting paid

Every invoice PkPay+ generates contains an embedded payment link. The customer opens the invoice, clicks **Pay Now**, and completes checkout using any payment method you support — cards, digital wallets, crypto, PIX, or Klarna BNPL. The payment lands in your PkPay+ balance instantly and the invoice is marked **Paid** in the dashboard in real time. No manual reconciliation or status updates are needed.

## Automated reminders

Stop chasing late payments. PkPay+ can automatically send polite, well-timed reminders on your behalf.

<Accordion title="Before the due date">
  Schedule a reminder to send a set number of days before the invoice is due. Use this to give customers advance notice and increase on-time payment rates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="On the due date">
  PkPay+ automatically sends a reminder on the exact due date if the invoice is still unpaid. The email includes the original payment link so customers can pay immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="After the due date">
  Configure one or more overdue reminders at intervals you define — for example, 3 days overdue, 7 days overdue, and 14 days overdue. Each reminder escalates in tone according to your template settings.
</Accordion>

## Auto-reconciliation

When a customer completes payment, PkPay+ marks the invoice **Paid** instantly and records the transaction against the invoice in your dashboard. For accounting workflows, you can sync invoice records directly to:

- **Xero** — automatic two-way sync of paid invoices and contacts
- **QuickBooks** — push invoice payments to your QuickBooks ledger automatically

## Invoice delivery options

Choose how your invoices reach customers:

| Method       | Description                                                                             |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email**    | Sent from PkPay+ SMTP on your behalf with your branding. Includes a one-click Pay Now button. |
| **Webhook**  | PkPay+ delivers a JSON payload to your endpoint when an invoice is created, viewed, or paid. |
| **Direct link** | Copy the invoice URL and share it manually via any channel — SMS, Slack, WhatsApp, etc. |

<Tip>
  Use multi-language invoices for international customers. PkPay+ supports invoice localisation, so the customer-facing invoice text renders in their preferred language while your dashboard always shows the canonical version.
</Tip>
