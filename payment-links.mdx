Payment Links let you collect money from customers without writing a single line of integration code. Create a link directly in the PkPay+ dashboard, share it anywhere you reach your audience, and PkPay+ takes care of the hosted checkout experience end to end.

## Creating a payment link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the merchant dashboard">
    Navigate to [merchant.pkpayplus.com](https://merchant.pkpayplus.com/) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Payment Links">
    In the left-hand sidebar, click **Payment Links** to open the links management screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Payment Link">
    Select **New Payment Link** in the top-right corner to open the link builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your link">
    Fill in the payment details:
    - **Amount** — fixed charge or let the customer enter their own amount
    - **Currency** — choose from 135+ supported currencies
    - **Description / Product name** — shown to the customer at checkout
    - **Success URL** — where to redirect the customer after a successful payment
    - **Failure URL** — where to redirect on a failed or cancelled payment
    - **Expiry date** — optionally set a date after which the link stops accepting payments
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and share the link">
    Click **Generate Link**. Copy the URL that appears — it is live and ready to share immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can customize

Every Payment Link supports the following configuration options:

- **Fixed or variable amount** — charge a set price or let the payer enter any amount
- **Currency** — any of the 135+ currencies supported by PkPay+
- **Description or product name** — displayed prominently on the checkout page
- **Success and failure redirect URLs** — control where the customer lands after checkout
- **Link expiry date** — automatically deactivate the link after a specific date
- **Optional product attachment** — link a product from your PkPay+ product catalogue

## Sharing your link

Once generated, you can distribute your Payment Link through any channel:

- **Email** — paste the link directly into a message or invoice email
- **SMS** — send a short payment request to a customer's phone
- **Embed as a button** — wrap the link in an anchor tag on any HTML page
- **Social media** — share on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram bio, or any platform
- **QR code** — generate a QR code from the link for print materials, invoices, or events

## Tracking payments

Every Payment Link has its own analytics view inside the dashboard. Select any link to see:

- **Views** — total times the checkout page was opened
- **Conversions** — number of successful payments
- **Total collected** — gross revenue attributed to this link
- **Individual transactions** — each payment with its timestamp, amount, and status

<Note>
  Payment Links use the same hosted checkout as the API integration. Every payment method supported by PkPay+ — including cards, digital wallets, cryptocurrencies, PIX, and Klarna BNPL — is available to your customers through a Payment Link.
</Note>
