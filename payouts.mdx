Once your customers pay, Paydios holds the collected funds in your merchant balance and then settles them to your bank account on a predictable schedule. This page explains how the payout process works, which fees are deducted before settlement, and how to configure your payout destination and schedule.

## How payouts work

1. **Customer completes a payment** — funds are captured and appear in your Paydios merchant balance.
2. **Funds are held in your merchant balance** — Paydios aggregates payments collected during the settlement period.
3. **Applicable fees are netted** — Paydios deducts processing and settlement fees from the gross balance before payout (see [Payout fees](#payout-fees) below).
4. **Net balance is transferred to your bank account** — per your configured payout schedule.

## Payout schedule

Startup and Professional plan merchants receive **next-day payouts** (T\+1) as standard — funds collected today are transferred to your bank account the next business day.

Enterprise plan merchants can negotiate a custom settlement schedule, including **instant payouts** or bespoke rolling windows tailored to your volume and risk profile.

<Note>
  Standard processing time is **T\+1 (next business day)**. Payouts initiated on a Friday settle on Monday. Public holidays in your bank's jurisdiction may add one additional business day.
</Note>

## Payout fees

The following fees are deducted from your gross balance before each payout. They apply across all plans in addition to the plan's processing fee.

| Fee Type | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Payout / Settlement Fee | 2.00% |
| Fraud & Risk Assessment Fee | 2.00% |
| GST / Tax Fee | 1.00% |
| Revenue Protection & Recovery | 1.50% |

For example, on a $$1,000 gross payout: $$20.00 settlement, $$20.00 fraud & risk, $$10.00 GST, and $$15.00 revenue protection are deducted, leaving a **$$935.00 net payout\*\* before the plan's transaction processing fee.

## Add a bank account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payout Settings">
    Log in to [merchant.paydios.io](https://merchant.paydios.io/) and navigate to **Settings → Payouts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Bank Account">
    Select **Add Bank Account** and choose your account type — domestic (routing \+ account number) or international (IBAN \+ BIC/SWIFT).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your account details">
    Provide the required bank account information:

    - **Account holder name** — must match the name on the bank account
    - **Routing number** (US) or **IBAN** (international)
    - **Account number** or **BIC/SWIFT code**
    - **Bank country and currency**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify via micro-deposit">
    Paydios sends two small micro-deposits (each under \$1.00) to your account within **1–2 business days**. Return to **Settings → Payouts** and enter the exact amounts to verify ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as default payout destination">
    Once verified, click **Set as Default** to make this account your primary settlement destination. You can add multiple accounts and switch between them at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track payouts

Open the **Payouts** tab in the dashboard to see a full history of every transfer. Each payout entry shows:

- **Status** — `scheduled`, `in_transit`, or `paid`
- **Gross amount** — total collected before fees
- **Net amount** — the amount actually transferred to your bank
- **Settlement date** — the date funds were sent
- **Linked transactions** — drill down to see every individual payment included in the payout

Use the date-range filter and CSV export to produce payout reports for your accounting records.
