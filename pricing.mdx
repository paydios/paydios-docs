## Enterprise Plan

For merchants processing more than \$1,000,000 USD per month, Paydios offers a fully customised commercial arrangement. Enterprise is not a fixed tier — rates, limits, and features are negotiated based on your volume, business model, and risk profile.

Enterprise includes everything in Professional, plus:

- No processing limits — no monthly volume ceiling
- Custom tailored volume-based transaction rates
- Custom negotiated Payout & Settlement Fees
- Custom Fraud & Risk Assessment rules tuned to your business
- Hosted Checkout, Payment Links & custom UI integration options
- Real-time enterprise analytics and custom reporting
- Instant or fully customisable payout schedules
- Multi-merchant and parent-child account setup for platforms and marketplaces
- Dedicated Integration & Support Engineering teams
- 24/7 SLA Support with a named Dedicated Account Manager

To discuss Enterprise pricing, contact the sales team at [support@paydios.io](mailto:support@paydios.io).

## Paydios vs Standard Processors

<Accordion title="Onboarding & Scale">
  Paydios offers fast merchant onboarding with dedicated compliance support — no long review cycles or unpredictable approvals. The platform is API-first and white-label ready, built for SaaS products, marketplaces, and platforms that need flexibility, not a one-size-fits-all gateway. Startup accounts scale to Enterprise without triggering account reviews or arbitrary volume holds.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Global Payments">
  Accept cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, cryptocurrencies, and local payment rails — including PIX for Brazil and Klarna BNPL for Europe and the US — all through a single integration. Paydios supports 135\+ currencies and 160\+ countries, with fast and transparent settlements. Standard processors often impose regional restrictions, delayed settlements, and unexpected payout holds.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Risk Management">
  Paydios uses advanced machine learning fraud protection and intelligent real-time monitoring tailored to your merchant profile. Generic rule-based risk engines at standard processors frequently block legitimate transactions or miss novel fraud patterns. With Paydios, fraud rules are dynamic and configurable per merchant.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Developer Experience">
  Integrate with the Paydios REST API, PHP SDK, Laravel package, or WordPress plugin in minutes. Webhooks, full API documentation, and SDK support are included on every plan. Standard processors often require complex custom integrations with limited flexibility and sparse documentation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Support">
  Every Paydios merchant gets a dedicated account manager — a real payment expert who understands your business. There are no automated bot queues or generic canned responses. Enterprise merchants receive 24/7 SLA-backed support with dedicated integration engineers.
</Accordion>
