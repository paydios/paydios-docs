## 1\. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your browser or device.

Cookies allow websites to recognize your browser, remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve security, and understand how users interact with a website.

Paydios may also use technologies similar to cookies, including:

- Pixels;
- Web beacons;
- Local storage;
- Device identifiers;
- Session technologies;
- Analytics technologies; and
- Similar tracking or measurement technologies.

For convenience, we refer to all of these technologies as “Cookies” in this Policy.

## 2\. How Paydios Uses Cookies

- Protect accounts;
- Detect suspicious activity;

- Improve website performance;
- Understand how users use our website;
- Measure traffic and engagement;
- Improve our Services;
- Troubleshoot technical issues;
- Analyze marketing performance; and
