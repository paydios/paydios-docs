## 2\. Definitions

**“Customer”** means a person or entity purchasing goods or services from a Merchant.

**“Payment Partner”** means any bank, acquiring institution, payment processor, payment network, card network, wallet provider, alternative payment provider, financial institution, crypto or digital-asset provider, or other third party used in connection with the Services.

**“Transaction”** means a payment or other payment-related transaction processed through the Services, including a payment, refund, reversal, dispute, or Chargeback.

**“Merchant Funds”** means funds collected or payable in connection with Transactions, less applicable fees, refunds, reversals, Chargebacks, Reserves, Holds, and other amounts owed.

**“Reserve”** means funds retained or restricted by Paydios to cover actual or anticipated Merchant obligations or financial exposure.

**“Hold”** means the temporary restriction or withholding of Merchant Funds for risk, compliance, dispute, refund, Chargeback, or other legitimate purposes.

**“Chargeback”** means a reversal or disputed Transaction initiated by a Customer, card issuer, bank, payment network, Payment Partner, or other applicable institution.

## 14\. Initial T+30 Settlement

Unless Paydios expressly provides otherwise in writing, newly approved Merchants will initially be subject to a **T+30 settlement schedule** .

Under T+30, eligible funds from a Transaction may become available for settlement approximately thirty (30) days after the applicable Transaction date.

T+30 remains subject to:

- Successful processing
- Reconciliation
- Applicable fees
- Refunds
- Chargebacks
- Reserves
- Holds
- Compliance reviews
- Payment Partner requirements
- Other applicable adjustments

T+30 is an initial risk-management arrangement and does not constitute a guarantee that funds will be released exactly thirty days after a Transaction.

- Chargebacks
- Refunds
- Reversals
- Customer complaints
- Disputes
- Fraud claims
- Network assessments
- Other payment-related liabilities

to arise and be resolved.

## 19\. Extension of Funds Holds

- Chargebacks
- Refunds

- Reversals
- Customer claims
- Fraud claims
- Network assessments
- Other liabilities

Where appropriate, Paydios may extend a Hold for additional periods of up to **180 days at a time** while unresolved or reasonably anticipated liabilities remain.

The expiration of an initial 180-day period does not require Paydios to release funds where doing so would leave Paydios reasonably exposed to an existing or anticipated obligation.
