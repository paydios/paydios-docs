## Prohibited uses of Paydios

These uses are not allowed in any industry.

| Activity | What this includes |
| --- | --- |
| False or incomplete business information | Misstating your identity, entity, products, website, or processing model. |
| Undisclosed merchants or products | Processing for another seller or a product that was not disclosed at onboarding. |
| Card testing or no real sale | Transactions with no bona fide good, service, or approved donation. |
| Virtual-terminal abuse | Using Paydios mainly to key-enter card data at scale. |
| Chargeback evasion | Avoiding card-network monitoring programs or dispute rules. |
| Sharing cardholder data | Passing payment data to another business for cross-sell. |
| Brand misuse | Using Paydios marks in a way that implies an untrue endorsement. |
