PkPay+ subscriptions let you charge customers on a recurring schedule — weekly, monthly, or annually — without any manual billing work. Once a subscriber signs up, PkPay+ handles every subsequent charge, retries failed payments automatically, and gives you real-time visibility into your recurring revenue.

## Subscription billing models

Choose the billing model that fits your business. PkPay+ supports all major recurring pricing structures out of the box.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flat-Rate" icon="dollar-sign">
    Charge a fixed amount every billing cycle — the simplest model for most SaaS and membership products. Every subscriber pays the same price regardless of usage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage-Based" icon="chart-line">
    Bill customers based on what they actually consume each cycle — ideal for API calls, seats activated, or data processed. Meters reset each billing period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tiered Pricing" icon="layer-group">
    Apply different rates at different volume thresholds. For example, the first 100 units at $0.10 each, the next 400 at $0.08, and everything above 500 at $0.05.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Seat" icon="users">
    Charge per user, licence, or active account. Perfect for team tools where the cost scales with the number of people using the product.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a subscription plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscriptions in the dashboard">
    Log in to [merchant.pkpayplus.com](https://merchant.pkpayplus.com/) and select **Subscriptions** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new plan">
    Click **New Plan** and configure:
    - **Plan name** — visible to subscribers at checkout
    - **Billing cycle** — weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual
    - **Amount and currency** — the recurring charge per cycle
    - **Trial period** — optional free trial before billing begins
    - **Billing model** — flat-rate, usage-based, tiered, or per-seat
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the subscribe link or integrate via API">
    Copy the hosted subscribe link to share directly, or use the API to embed the subscription sign-up flow in your own application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribers bill automatically">
    PkPay+ charges each subscriber on their renewal date and updates their status in real time. You receive a dashboard notification and transaction record for every successful charge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing subscribers

From the Subscriptions dashboard you can take the following actions on any active subscriber:

- **Upgrade or downgrade plan** — move a subscriber to a different plan with automatic proration applied
- **Apply proration** — credit or charge the difference when changing plans mid-cycle
- **Pause subscription** — temporarily suspend billing without cancelling the subscription
- **Cancel at end of period** — keep access active until the current period expires, then stop billing
- **Cancel immediately** — terminate the subscription and stop billing right away
- **View billing history** — see every invoice, charge, and refund for each subscriber

## Smart dunning & recovery

Failed payments are one of the biggest drivers of involuntary churn. PkPay+'s smart dunning engine minimises that loss automatically.

- **Optimised retry logic** — PkPay+ retries failed charges at intervals calibrated to maximise recovery without annoying the customer
- **Card update reminders** — automated emails prompt subscribers to update expired or replaced card details before a charge fails
- **Escalation rules** — configure how many retry attempts to make before downgrading or cancelling an account
- **Recovery rate** — PkPay+'s dunning engine achieves a **99.4% dunning success rate** for merchants with active retry rules enabled

## Subscription analytics

Track your recurring revenue health directly from the dashboard. The following metrics are available in real time:

- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)** and **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**
- **LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)** by plan and cohort
- **Churn rate** — monthly and rolling averages
- **Active subscriber cohorts** — retention curves from sign-up date
- **Financial reporting** — exportable breakdowns for accounting and forecasting
